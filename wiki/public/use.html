Use

Open the player and click Downloads in the library. It has two tabs.

Find songs

  • Type a song or artist and press Enter. Results come from YouTube Music, official releases first; slowed, sped-up, lyric and cover uploads sink unless you ask for them. If YouTube Music can't be reached, YouTube is searched instead.
  • Download the top card or a row's arrow. A tick means you have it already. Sort, Show more (up to 30) and past-search buttons sit above the list.
  • An artist search puts an Artist card first, with Download all.
  • Paste a link instead and press Enter. Cancel stops it and keeps what finished. Missing tools are named, with the command to install them.
  • Spotify songs are matched to YouTube Music. Unclear matches (remix, live, different length) wait for Keep, Skip or Skip all.

Downloaded

Your songs, with a filter box. Right-click → Delete download, or select several and press Delete. Open folder opens the pack.

Commands

CommandDoes
/cmm download <link>Downloads it; progress shows above the hotbar.
/cmm download cancelStops it; also answers a pending match with Skip.
/cmm downloadsLists songs. Click the pack name to open its folder.
/cmm downloads remove <song>Deletes one.

Rules

  • One download at a time. Live streams and anything over an hour are skipped, as are private, removed or region-locked videos in a playlist.
  • watch?v=…&list=… downloads the one video; use playlist?list=… for the list.
  • Title and artist come from the site's track data, else the video title and uploader ("Artist - Topic" becomes "Artist"). Spotify keeps Spotify's.
  • Downloading a song again replaces it; artist and Spotify links skip songs you have.
  • Each download or removal reads only the song list again, so the song that's on keeps playing. The first download turns the pack on, which reloads resources and stops what's playing.
  • With Cue My Music's defaults, downloads automix (matched loudness, blends up to 8 s) and stay out of game-audio recordings (rec-util, OBS app capture). Both are in its Settings.
