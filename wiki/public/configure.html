Configure

There's no settings screen.

Settings

Songs are saved in resourcepacks/Cue My Music Downloads/, a normal folder you can copy or share. Delete it to reset.

SettingDefaultNotes
PackOnAt the bottom of your list. Turn it off under Resource Packs.
Sound qualityOgg Vorbis 10About 400 kbps, so a 5-minute song is about 15 MB.

Homebrew's ffmpeg lacks libvorbis, so on a Mac install vorbis-tools. Without either, a weaker encoder is used and the Downloads page warns.
