Cue My Music: Downloads

Find songs by name or link and save them into a resource pack for Cue My Music.

Search for a song by name on the Downloads page of the player, or paste a link there or into /cmm download <link>, and the song is downloaded with yt-dlp, converted to Ogg with ffmpeg, and added to a resource pack called Cue My Music Downloads. The pack is turned on for you, and the song shows up on the add-on's Downloads page in the player.

A playlist link downloads the whole playlist (up to 200 songs) and makes a Cue My Music playlist with the same name. An artist link downloads the artist's channel (up to 1000 songs) and adds them under the artist that Cue My Music already groups them by; downloading the artist again adds only the new songs. Spotify songs, albums, playlists and artists work too, through spotdl, which reads the Spotify metadata while the audio still comes from YouTube.

Downloaded songs are add-on songs: they play in Cue My Music's Add-ons mode, which picks them in place of the game's music. Vanilla + FX leaves them out.

Only download what you have the right to. Many sites don't allow it in their terms.
