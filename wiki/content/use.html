Use

Open the player and click Downloads in the library.

Find songs

  • Type a song or artist and press Enter. Results come from YouTube Music, official releases first.
  • Click the top card or a row's arrow. A tick means you have the song.
  • An artist search puts an Artist card first, with Download all.
  • Or paste a link and press Enter. Cancel stops it and keeps what finished.
  • Unclear Spotify matches wait for Keep, Skip or Skip all.

Downloaded

Your songs, with a filter box. Right-click → Delete download, or select several and press Delete. Open folder opens the pack.

Commands

CommandDoes
/cmm download <link>Downloads it; progress shows above the hotbar.
/cmm download cancelStops it; also answers a pending match with Skip.
/cmm downloadsLists songs. Click the pack name to open its folder.
/cmm downloads remove <song>Deletes one.

Rules

  • One download at a time. Live streams and anything over an hour are skipped, as are private, removed or region-locked videos in a playlist.
  • watch?v=…&list=… downloads the one video; use playlist?list=… for the list.
  • Downloading a song again replaces it; artist and Spotify links skip songs you have.
  • The first download turns the pack on, which stops what's playing. Later ones don't.
