The pack

A normal folder in resourcepacks: copy or share it as is. Delete it, or turn it off under Resource Packs, to remove everything.

resourcepacks/Cue My Music Downloads/
  pack.mcmeta
  assets/cuemymusic_dl/
    sounds.json        one sound event, cuemymusic_dl:music.downloads
    cuemymusic.json    each song's title, artist, album, and link
    sounds/music/*.ogg named by video id

Sound quality

Minecraft plays only Ogg Vorbis, so songs are converted at libvorbis quality 10 (about 400 kbps, 15 MB for 5 minutes). The source is already about 130 kbps Opus; any lower and drums smear audibly.

Windows and Linux ffmpeg include libvorbis. Homebrew's doesn't: install vorbis-tools and its oggenc is used. Without either, ffmpeg's weaker built-in encoder is used and the Downloads page warns; download again after fixing it.
