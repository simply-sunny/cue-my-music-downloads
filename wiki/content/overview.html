Cue My Music: Downloads

Find songs by name or link and save them into a resource pack for Cue My Music.

Search on the player's Downloads page, or paste a YouTube or Spotify link. yt-dlp downloads it, ffmpeg converts it to Ogg, and it lands in a resource pack called Cue My Music Downloads, which is turned on for you.

LinkDownloads
Song or videoThat song.
Playlist or albumUp to 200 songs, plus a Cue My Music playlist of the same name.
Artist or channelUp to 1000 songs under that artist. Again later adds only new ones.
Spotify (any of the above)Spotify's metadata through spotdl, audio from YouTube.

Downloads are add-on songs: they play in Cue My Music's Add-ons mode only. Only download what you have the right to.

You install yt-dlp, ffmpeg and spotdl: the add-on doesn't include or download them. It contacts YouTube Music for searches and YouTube for thumbnails, and iTunes for album covers. The tools contact the sites in the links you give them.
