Cue My Music: Downloads

Download songs into Cue My Music by name or link.

Search on the player's Downloads page, or paste a link. Songs go into a resource pack and play in Cue My Music's Add-ons mode.

LinkDownloads
Song or videoThat song.
Playlist or albumUp to 200 songs, plus a Cue My Music playlist of the same name.
Artist or channelUp to 1000 songs under that artist. Again later adds only new ones.
Spotify (any of the above)Spotify's metadata through spotdl, audio from YouTube.

You install yt-dlp, ffmpeg and spotdl. The add-on doesn't bundle or download them. It contacts YouTube Music, and YouTube and iTunes for covers. Only download what you have the right to.
