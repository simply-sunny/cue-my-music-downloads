Cue My Music: Downloads

Download songs into Cue My Music by name or link.

Search on the player's Downloads page, or paste a link. Songs go into a resource pack and play in Cue My Music's Add-ons mode.

You install yt-dlp, ffmpeg and spotdl. The add-on doesn't bundle or download them. It contacts YouTube Music, and YouTube and iTunes for covers. Only download what you have the right to. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Mojang, Microsoft, YouTube, Spotify or Apple.
