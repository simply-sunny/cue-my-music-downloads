Troubleshooting

Problem Fix
Couldn't find yt-dlp or ffmpeg Install it (the page shows the command), or link it into /usr/local/bin or ~/.local/bin.
Couldn't find spotdl, or a Spotify link says it needs it pipx install spotdl
A Spotify song matched the wrong video Press Skip (or Skip all) at the question.
Download failed from YouTube Update yt-dlp (brew upgrade yt-dlp, winget upgrade yt-dlp.yt-dlp, or yt-dlp -U). Sites change often.
The song isn't in the player Check that Cue My Music Downloads is on under Resource Packs.

yt-dlp's full output for a failed download is in logs/latest.log.
