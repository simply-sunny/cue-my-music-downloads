Troubleshooting

Problem Fix
requires cuemymusic Install Cue My Music 1.4.0 or newer.
yt-dlp isn't installed or ffmpeg isn't installed Install it (the page shows the command), or link it into /usr/local/bin or ~/.local/bin.
Spotify links need spotdl Run pipx install spotdl. No restart needed.
A Spotify song matched the wrong video Press Skip or Skip all at Is this the right song?
Download failed from YouTube Update yt-dlp (brew upgrade yt-dlp, winget upgrade yt-dlp.yt-dlp, or yt-dlp -U). Sites change often.
The song isn't in the player Check that Cue My Music Downloads is on under Resource Packs.

Report issues with logs/latest.log and your Minecraft, mods and loader versions. It holds yt-dlp's full output.

Limits

  • One download at a time. Live streams and anything over an hour are skipped, as are private, removed or region-locked videos in a playlist.
  • watch?v=…&list=… downloads the one video; use playlist?list=… for the list.
  • Downloading a song again replaces it; artist and Spotify links skip songs you have.
  • The first download turns the pack on, which stops what's playing. Later ones don't.
